Source: Worldpay, The Global Payments Report 2026 (11th edition). Payment method shares are % of 2025 transaction value and card scheme shares are for 2024 (the latest the report gives); both were read from the report by script, and totals may not equal 100% because of rounding. Dollar values for payment methods are our own arithmetic: the 2025 share times the report's 2025 market size. Card schemes get no dollar value because their shares are for 2024. Account, debit card and digital payment figures are from the World Bank Global Findex Database (2024 survey round where available, otherwise 2021). The clusters are our own k-means grouping of those figures and the report's, not something either source publishes. Brands and logos are the "popular payment methods" the report shows for each market, and are trademarks of their owners. The one-line description under each brand is ours, not the report's.
